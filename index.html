Chapter 4The month

Here is one month on the three-piece plan, as it might go for you.

Your monthDay 01 of 30
Delivered Last week Due back
  1. Day 01Delivered

    The box arrives

    A courier hands it over in person, at the slot you booked. Inside are the three pieces you chose in the app.

    Knot ring, Petal drop earrings, Marquise spray pendant

  2. Day 03Worn

    An ordinary morning

    The pendant goes on with a white shirt. No occasion needed. For a month, these are simply yours to wear.

  3. Day 12Swapped

    Something new catches your eye

    Swaps opened on day 2. You pick the Double rivière in the app and book a slot. The courier collects the ring and delivers the necklace.

    Double rivière in, Knot ring out

  4. Day 24Extended

    The last week

    The pendant is due back on day 30, and you are not ready to let it go. One tap in the app extends it another 30 days.

    Marquise spray pendant, extended 30 days

  5. Day 30Returned

    A different box

    The earrings go back. The pendant stays. A slot opens for whatever you choose next.

    Petal drop earrings returned, one slot open

After day 30Month two

Then it starts again, with different diamonds

Swap as often as you like from day 2. The rules are the same on every plan.
